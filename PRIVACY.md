# Privacy Policy — Bunny

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Bunny is an Android YouTube client developed by an independent
developer. Bunny has **no server of its own**: the app talks
directly from your device to Google/YouTube, and everything
personal stays on your device.

## What Bunny accesses

- **Without sign-in:** only public YouTube data (videos, channels,
  search results), fetched anonymously.
- **With Google sign-in (optional):** Bunny asks for the
  read-only scope
  `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly`. With it, the
  app can read your channel identity, your subscriptions, videos
  from those subscriptions, and your liked videos. Bunny never
  posts, deletes, or modifies anything in your account.

## What Bunny stores, and where

- Sign-in tokens are stored **only on your device**, encrypted
  with a key held in the Android Keystore. They are never sent
  anywhere except to Google's own endpoints to keep your session
  alive, and you can wipe them at any time with the Sign out
  button (which also revokes the grant at Google).
- Subscriptions you create inside Bunny (local subscriptions),
  your watch history inside Bunny, downloads, and settings are
  local app data on your device only.
- Bunny collects **no analytics**, shows **no ads**, and contains
  **no third-party trackers**. The developer receives no usage
  data from the app.

## Sharing

Bunny shares nothing with the developer or any third party,
because Bunny has no backend to share it with. Network requests
go only to Google/YouTube services (and, for app configuration,
a public configuration file hosted on GitHub).
